Welcome to **Hétu**. This project is a digital experiment in radical transparency and self-sustaining creativity. 

We have moved beyond a simple donation model into what I call the **Trinity Model**. For this project to be eternal, it must balance three specific energies:

1. **Rajas (The Music):** The active creation. I release music and its underlying Ableton files under a **CC0 (Public Domain)** license. It is meant to be used, sampled, and reimagined by you.
2. **Sattva (The Impact):** The pure result. **80%** of all artist royalties are routed automatically to our four charity partners: *Action Against Hunger, All Hands & Hearts, Miracle Messages,* and *Mission Blue.*
3. **Tamas (The Foundation):** The necessary sustenance. **20%** of revenue is retained to cover project overheads—subscriptions, instruments, and storage. This ensures the loop never breaks due to external financial pressure.

This project belongs to the public. The loops are infinite. The impact is real.
